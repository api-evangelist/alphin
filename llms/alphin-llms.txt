# alphin

> alphin (alphin GmbH, formerly Freachly GmbH, Berlin) is a local-marketing platform for
> small businesses — restaurants, cafes, salons and similar SMBs — delivered as an
> all-in-one web and mobile Business App. It does not publish a public API, developer
> portal, SDKs or webhooks.

alphin consolidates online-presence management for local businesses into a single
dashboard with daily reporting and analytics. Founded 2016, serving 1,000+ local
businesses across Germany, Austria, the UK and Israel. Backed by HV Capital, Partech,
Scale Capital and Wille Finance.

## Products

- [alphin Reviews](https://www.alphin.com/products-en/review-management): review collection and management across Google, TripAdvisor, Yelp and other review platforms.
- [alphin Socials](https://www.alphin.com/products-en/instagram-management): Instagram and social account management, full service.
- [alphin Photos](https://www.alphin.com/products-en/photo): professional photography, shot and distributed to the business's profiles.
- [alphin Local Influencers](https://www.alphin.com/products-en/influencer-marketing): automated local influencer marketing against a pool of verified local-reach influencers.
- [alphin Local Ads](https://www.alphin.com/products-en/instagram-ads): targeted Instagram advertising for a local radius.
- [alphin Portals](https://www.alphin.com/products-en/online-profile-management): multi-platform business-profile management across Google, TripAdvisor and Yelp.

## Developer surface

alphin has no developer program. As of 2026-08-12:

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL, AsyncAPI or gRPC contract is published on any alphin host.
- No developer portal, API reference or getting-started guide exists; developer.alphin.com,
  docs.alphin.com and docs.alphin.io do not resolve.
- No first-party SDK or client library is published to npm, PyPI, RubyGems, crates.io or
  any other registry.
- No public GitHub organization.
- `api.alphin.io` is the private backend serving the alphin Business App single-page
  application. Its root and every well-known path return 404; `/v1` returns 401 with an
  empty body. It is not offered to third-party developers and no contract for it is published.
- No `/.well-known/` document is served on any alphin host. Requests to
  `app.alphin.io/.well-known/*` return HTTP 200 with the Angular SPA shell, not a document.
- No public pricing: commercial engagement is through a demo request.
